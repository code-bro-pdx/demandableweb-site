# The Demandable Web Project - What the heck is Demandable Web?

Demandable Web is a decentralized, blockchain-powered marketplace platform designed to bridge the gap between providers and consumers of home and professional services. 

## Our Vision
To redefine the digital service economy by leveraging distributed ledger technology to foster a transparent, efficient, and equitable ecosystem where trust is inherent and barriers to entry are minimized.

## Our Mission
Our mission is to empower individuals and professionals alike by providing a secure, decentralized environment for service exchange. We aim to modernize the way service-based transactions occur, prioritizing user autonomy and community-driven verification.

## Project Goals
- **Decentralization:** Removing central intermediaries to ensure service providers retain greater value while users benefit from competitive pricing.
- **Accessibility:** Providing a user-friendly interface that facilitates seamless connections between local service needs and global professional talent.
- **Integrity:** Utilizing blockchain architecture to provide an immutable and verifiable history of service interactions.

## Getting Started
The Demandable Web platform is accessible at [www.demandableweb.io](http://www.demandableweb.io). The site is currently powered by Grav CMS and deployed via static hosting services provided by Porkbun.
